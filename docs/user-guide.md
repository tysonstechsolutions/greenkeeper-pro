## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

GreenKeeper Pro works on any device with a web browser - your phone, tablet, or computer.

**To access the app:**
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge)
2. Go to your course's GreenKeeper Pro URL (ask your superintendent for this)
3. You'll see the login screen

### Installing as a Mobile App (PWA)

For the best mobile experience, install GreenKeeper Pro as an app on your phone:

**On iPhone (iOS):**
1. Open the app in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen

**On Android:**
1. Open the app in Chrome
2. Tap the menu (three dots)
3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
4. Confirm by tapping "Install"
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen

### Logging In

1. Enter your email address (provided by your superintendent)
2. Enter your password
3. Tap "Sign In"

**First time logging in?**
- Check your email for a welcome message with your temporary password
- You'll be prompted to change your password on first login
- Create a password that's easy to remember but hard to guess

### Understanding Your Role

Your access level depends on your role:

- **Superintendent**: Full access to all features, can manage staff and settings
- **Assistant Superintendent**: Most features, can create tasks and manage schedules
- **Foreman**: Can manage crew tasks and view reports
- **Mechanic**: Focus on equipment management and maintenance
- **Crew Member**: View and complete assigned tasks, update equipment status
- **Seasonal**: Limited access for seasonal staff

Your role badge appears next to your name in the app.

## Core Features

### Tasks

Tasks are the heart of daily operations. Every maintenance activity is tracked as a task.

#### Viewing Tasks

1. Tap "Tasks" in the bottom navigation bar
2. You'll see three tabs:
   - **My Tasks**: Tasks assigned to you
   - **All Tasks**: All team tasks (managers can see this)
   - **Overdue**: Tasks past their due date

**What You See on Each Task:**
- Task title and description
- Priority level (Critical, High, Normal, Low)
- Status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, etc.)
- Category (Mowing, Irrigation, Chemical, etc.)
- Assigned person
- Due date and time
- Location on course

#### Completing a Task

1. Tap on the task from your list
2. Review the task details
3. If there's a checklist, mark items as you complete them
4. Take before/after photos if required
5. Add notes about what you did
6. Tap "Mark as Complete"

**Tips:**
- Start tasks when you begin work by changing status to "In Progress"
- Take photos to document your work
- Add notes about any issues or observations
- If you can't complete a task, mark it as "Blocked" and explain why

#### Filtering Tasks

Use filters to find specific tasks:
1. Tap the "Filters" button
2. Search by task name
3. Filter by:
   - Date
   - Category (Mowing, Irrigation, etc.)
   - Priority level
   - Status
   - Assigned person

### Schedule

Check your work schedule and see when you're expected on the course.

#### Viewing Your Schedule

1. Tap "Schedule" in the bottom navigation
2. Select "My Schedule" tab
3. You'll see your shifts for the week:
   - Days you're working
   - Shift times (Morning, Afternoon, Full Day, etc.)
   - Days off
   - Any approved time off

**Shift Types:**
- **Morning**: Early shift (typically 5 AM - 1 PM)
- **Afternoon**: Late shift (typically 12 PM - 8 PM)
- **Full Day**: Standard 8-hour shift
- **Split**: Work two separate periods in one day
- **On Call**: Available if needed

#### Requesting Time Off

1. Tap "Schedule" > "My Schedule"
2. Tap "Request Time Off"
3. Select your dates
4. Choose type (Vacation, Sick, Personal)
5. Add notes if needed
6. Submit request
7. Wait for superintendent approval

Your supervisor will approve or deny requests. You'll get a notification when they respond.

### Equipment

Track equipment status and report issues.

#### Viewing Equipment

1. Tap "More" > "Equipment"
2. Browse the equipment list
3. Use the search bar to find specific machines
4. Filter by:
   - Equipment type (Mowers, Sprayers, Utility Vehicles, etc.)
   - Status (Operational, Needs Service, In Repair, etc.)

**What You See:**
- Equipment name and photo
- Make, model, and year
- Current status
- Hour meter reading
- Hours until next service

#### Reporting Equipment Issues

1. Find the equipment in the list
2. Tap to open details
3. Tap "Report Issue"
4. Describe the problem
5. Add photos if helpful
6. Submit the report

Mechanics and supervisors will get notified immediately.

#### Updating Hour Meters

Help keep maintenance on schedule:
1. Open the equipment details
2. Tap "Update Hours"
3. Enter the current hour meter reading
4. Save

This helps prevent breakdowns by ensuring timely maintenance.

### Chemical Applications

**IMPORTANT**: Accurate chemical records are required by law. Take your time and be precise.

#### Logging an Application

1. Tap "Chemicals" in the main menu
2. Tap "Log Application"
3. Fill in all required information:
   - **Product**: Select from inventory
   - **Zones Applied**: Choose all areas treated
   - **Rate**: Amount applied per area
   - **Total Amount**: Total product used
   - **Target**: What you're treating (weeds, disease, pests)
   - **Weather**: Conditions during application
   - **Applied By**: Your name (auto-filled)
4. Review all information carefully
5. Submit

**Why This Matters:**
- Required for EPA compliance
- Creates restricted entry interval (REI) zones
- Tracks product inventory
- Records applicator certification

**REI - Restricted Entry Interval:**
When you apply certain products, areas become restricted. The app automatically:
- Posts REI warnings in the system
- Notifies staff to stay out of treated areas
- Counts down remaining restricted hours

Always check for active REIs before entering any zone.

### Daily Briefing

Get AI-powered morning updates about course conditions and priorities.

#### Viewing the Daily Briefing

1. Tap "More" > "Daily Briefing"
2. Read the sections:
   - **Weather Summary**: Today's forecast and concerns
   - **Priority Areas**: Zones needing attention
   - **Recommended Actions**: Suggested tasks
   - **Staff Notes**: Important reminders

The briefing is generated fresh each morning based on:
- Weather forecasts
- Current course conditions
- Recent issues
- Seasonal priorities

Your superintendent reviews and approves each briefing before you see it.

## Communication

### Messages

Stay connected with your team through built-in messaging.

#### Starting a Conversation

1. Tap "More" > "Messages"
2. Tap "New Message"
3. Choose:
   - **Direct Message**: Private chat with one person
   - **New Group**: Create a group channel

**For Direct Messages:**
1. Search for the team member
2. Tap their name
3. Start chatting

**For Group Channels:**
1. Name your group (e.g., "Greens Team")
2. Select members
3. Tap "Create Group"

#### Sending Messages

- Type your message in the text box
- Tap the send button (arrow icon)
- Press Enter on keyboard to send (Shift+Enter for new line)

**Attachments:**
- **Camera icon**: Take a photo
- **Paperclip icon**: Attach an existing photo
- **# icon**: Link to a specific task

#### Viewing Messages

- Unread messages show a badge with the count
- Recent messages appear at the bottom
- Scroll up to see older messages
- Tap "Load older messages" if needed

**Message Actions:**
- Long-press or tap menu to edit/delete your messages
- Pin important messages
- React with emoji (if enabled)

### Notifications

Stay informed about important updates.

#### What You'll Be Notified About

- Tasks assigned to you
- Schedule changes
- Equipment issues
- Chemical REI warnings
- Messages from teammates
- System announcements

#### Managing Notifications

1. Tap "Settings" > "Notifications"
2. Choose what you want to receive:
   - Push notifications (on your device)
   - Email notifications
3. Set quiet hours if you don't want notifications at night
4. Save your preferences

**Tips:**
- Keep task notifications on so you don't miss assignments
- Enable push notifications for the best experience
- Check notifications regularly if you disable push

## Mobile & Offline Use

GreenKeeper Pro works even when you're out on the course with spotty cell service.

### Working Offline

**What Works Offline:**
- View your tasks
- View your schedule
- Take photos
- Add notes to tasks
- Mark tasks as complete

**What Needs Internet:**
- Loading new data
- Sending messages
- Creating new tasks
- Chemical applications (requires real-time tracking)

### How Offline Mode Works

1. When you lose internet, the app switches to offline mode automatically
2. You'll see an "Offline" indicator
3. Continue working - your changes are saved locally
4. When you reconnect, everything syncs automatically
5. You'll get a confirmation when sync is complete

**Tips for Offline Use:**
- Open the app with internet before going to remote areas
- This loads your tasks and data
- Take photos - they'll upload when you reconnect
- Don't worry about losing work - it's saved on your device

### Installing as a Progressive Web App (PWA)

Installing the app makes offline mode more reliable:

**Benefits:**
- Works more like a native app
- Better offline support
- Faster loading
- Takes up less space than traditional apps
- Always up to date (no manual updates needed)

See the "Installing as a Mobile App" section at the top of this guide for installation instructions.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Can't Log In

**Problem**: "Invalid email or password" error

**Solutions:**
1. Double-check your email address for typos
2. Make sure Caps Lock is off
3. Try the "Forgot Password" link
4. Contact your superintendent if still unable to login

#### Tasks Not Loading

**Problem**: Task list is empty or won't refresh

**Solutions:**
1. Pull down on the task list to refresh
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try closing and reopening the app
4. Tap the refresh button
5. Sign out and sign back in

#### Photos Won't Upload

**Problem**: Photos stuck in "uploading" state

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Make sure you have enough phone storage
3. Try taking a new photo
4. Close other apps to free up memory
5. Wait a few minutes - large files take time

#### Notifications Not Working

**Problem**: Not receiving push notifications

**Solutions:**
1. Check your notification settings in the app
2. Check your phone's notification settings:
   - **iPhone**: Settings > Notifications > GreenKeeper Pro
   - **Android**: Settings > Apps > GreenKeeper Pro > Notifications
3. Make sure Do Not Disturb is off
4. Try signing out and back in

#### App Running Slowly

**Problem**: App is laggy or freezing

**Solutions:**
1. Close other apps running on your device
2. Close and reopen GreenKeeper Pro
3. Clear your browser cache (if using browser)
4. Update to the latest browser version
5. Restart your device

#### Offline Sync Issues

**Problem**: Changes not syncing when back online

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you have a stable internet connection
2. Open the app and wait a few minutes
3. Pull down to manually refresh
4. Check for a sync status indicator
5. If problems persist, contact support before making more changes

### Getting Help

**If you can't solve an issue:**

1. **Ask Your Supervisor**
   - They may have encountered the same problem
   - Can check if there's a known issue

2. **Check for Updates**
   - Make sure you're using the latest version
   - Browser updates may fix issues

3. **Contact Your Superintendent**
   - They can check your account settings
   - May need to contact technical support on your behalf

4. **Document the Problem**
   - Take screenshots if possible
   - Note what you were doing when the error occurred
   - Write down any error messages

### Tips for Best Performance

**Keep the App Running Smoothly:**
- Update your browser regularly
- Keep your phone's operating system up to date
- Clear browser cache monthly
- Close the app completely when not in use
- Restart your device weekly

**Battery Saving Tips:**
- Lower screen brightness when outside
- Close the app when not actively using it
- Use Wi-Fi instead of cellular when possible
- Enable low power mode on long days

**Data Usage Tips:**
- Download data while on Wi-Fi before heading out
- Limit photo uploads until on Wi-Fi
- Work offline when on the far reaches of the course
- Sync at the maintenance building with good Wi-Fi

## Glossary

**Terms You'll See in the App:**

- **Dashboard**: Your home screen with overview information
- **Task**: A work assignment with details and tracking
- **Zone**: A defined area of the course (green, fairway, rough, etc.)
- **REI (Restricted Entry Interval)**: Time after chemical application when entry is restricted
- **Status**: Current state of a task or equipment (pending, in progress, completed, etc.)
- **Priority**: Urgency level of a task (critical, high, normal, low)
- **Shift**: Your scheduled work period
- **PTO**: Paid Time Off
- **Hour Meter**: Equipment usage tracking (like an odometer)
- **Service Interval**: Hours between required maintenance
- **Channel**: A message conversation (direct or group)
- **Notification**: An alert about important updates
- **Offline Mode**: Working without internet connection
- **Sync**: Uploading local changes to the server
